## Assumptions and design
Only positive axis-aligned affine scale/translation below the list surface; no rotation/shear/perspective/flip, sublayer transform, mask, rounded clipping, hidden ancestor, cumulative alpha<0.99 or existing layer animation. Traverse the ENTIRE source->window chain (max64), confirm actual surface and window membership, intersect every rectangular clipping ancestor plus the list surface and window bounds in surface coordinates. Derive source-local crop with proper inverse conversion, round it INWARD on the capture pixel grid, and capture only that crop. Never shift the original card, reveal cropped-away content, ignore CSScrollView or guess displacement from a boolean. Snapshot crop maps to its real position in the list; particle texture coordinates refer only to the cropped image. A fixed rectangular overlay mask restricts particle travel to the common allowed area. Final tiny/empty/invalid regions fail before original animation suppression. Surface->window geometry and policies are checkpointed and revalidated at commit and during lifetime, without retaining removed notification views. Preflight must match existing stricter commit constraints so known-ineligible transforms cannot suppress original animation.

## Success criteria and independent failure signals
Observed 183pt-left-clipped card yields a cropped187x80 source region (subject to other ancestors and inward pixel rounding), positioned at x0,y521 instead of restoring the370pt card. Nested clip intersections all apply even after the first partial ancestor. No snapshot pixel or particle texture comes from the hidden part; no motion outside shared allowed clip region. Source bounds origin, scroll bounds origin and scale do not double-offset texture. Unknown/nonrect/angular cases, empty visibility, source still attached after removal, changed/reparented surface chain or original exception remain fail-closed. Original each wrapper exactly once; prepare nil preserves animated/displaced, duplicate overload nesting does not capture twice. No delayed model deletion/reinsertion/system-cell writes/global overlay window; no new personal data, images on disk or network diagnostics.
