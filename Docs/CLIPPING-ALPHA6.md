## Diagnostic field legend
- All rectangles are `(x,y,width,height)` in points, rounded to 0.01pt. Coordinates retain signs and clamp to +/-32768 with `clamped=1`; `invalid` is not a coordinate sentinel.
- `source_bounds` is the selected controller root's own bounds; `source_in_ancestor` is that full rectangle in the rejected ancestor's local coordinates; `ancestor_bounds` includes its scroll origin.
- `contains_half_pt` is the unrounded result of the original 0.5pt containment predicate. `local_intersection` and `local_overlap_permille` describe only this ancestor's exact bounds, not all-ancestor visibility or a safe capture region. The overlap ratio uses normalized axis ratios to avoid area-product underflow/overflow. Invalid geometry yields `valid=0` with ratio/containment `-1`.
- `edge_overflow_ltrb` gives left, top, right, bottom excess against the exact bounds (before tolerance). A non-clipping rejection atomically replaces and clears the preceding geometry.
